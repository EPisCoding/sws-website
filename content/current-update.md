## Our story so far

[Photo: images/grandad-standing.jpg]
[Alt: "Euan Piercy standing beside his grandad, who is holding the Smart Walking Stick prototype at home."]

**The Smart Walking Stick started with my grandad.**

Watching him live with Parkinson's, I wanted to build something designed to support everyday walking, especially in the moments when your feet just won't move. What began as my university dissertation at Swansea University is now a working walking stick with three cues built into the handle: a rhythmic beat, a gentle vibration and a laser line on the ground to step towards.

My grandad has been part of it from the start, trying out each version and telling me exactly what he thinks.

*Euan Piercy, Founder*

## Where we are now

- **The second-generation prototype is complete.** It is fully working, with all three cues running on battery and a companion app for settings.
- **Shaped by people with Parkinson's.** The first prototype was shown to more than 30 members of a Parkinson's support group, and the new handle and button positions were shaped by feedback from people living with Parkinson's.
- **Building a small batch.** We're building a small number of sticks for demonstrations and for feedback from healthcare professionals.
- **Research at Swansea University.** I've started a Master's by Research at Swansea University, which will include carefully planned, ethically approved user testing.
- **Thank you.** We're grateful for advice and support from Swansea University's Enterprise team, the Parkinson's community and local business networks.
