Privacy

This page explains what this website does with your information. The short version: very little.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Euan Piercy. If you have a question about your information, email hello@smartwalkingstick.co.uk.

Visiting this website

This website sets no cookies. It uses no analytics and no tracking.

The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which also receives your IP address, as any web host does.

Updates by email

If you sign up on our Stay in touch page, we collect your email address and, if you choose, your first name. Your browser sends them straight to Zoho Campaigns, the service that sends our emails, and they are stored there. This website never receives or stores them.

Zoho then emails you to confirm. You are only added to the list once you confirm.

We use them only to send occasional updates about the project. We never sell or share them.

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. Or email hello@smartwalkingstick.co.uk and we'll delete your details.

Donations

Donations are handled by Stripe. We can see your name, your email address and the amount in Stripe. Only the overall total appears on this site.

Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see them.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Updated October 2026.

Get in touch
